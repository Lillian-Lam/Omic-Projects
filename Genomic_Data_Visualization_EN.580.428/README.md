## Overview

This project applies spatially resolved transcriptomics (10x Visium) to characterize the transcriptional response of male mouse kidneys to bilateral ischemia-reperfusion injury (Bi-IRI).
By comparing tissue collected at baseline (Sham), 4 hours, and 12 hours post-injury, we map where injury occurs, which cell types are affected, how immune cells are recruited, and what signaling pathways drive damage versus repair.

## Biological Background

Bilateral ischemia-reperfusion injury (Bi-IRI) is induced by clamping both renal pedicles for 22 minutes in male mice, cutting off blood flow to the kidneys and then restoring it. This models the kind of acute kidney injury (AKI) that can occur in surgical or clinical settings.

**Timepoints collected for analysis:**
- **Sham (0h):** Surgery performed without actual clamping, which serves as the healthy baseline control.
- **AKI 4h:** Early acute phase, 4 hours after clamp removal.
- **AKI 12h:** Peak acute injury phase, 12 hours after clamp removal.

Each kidney was bisected coronally and prepared for Visium spatial transcriptomics.

## Data

Data were sourced from the Dixon Paper (https://journals.lww.com/jasn/fulltext/2022/02000/spatially_resolved_transcriptomic_analysis_of.5.aspx) and loaded as `.csv.gz` files:

## Methods & Analysis Pipeline

### 1. Quality Control & Preprocessing
- Raw counts filtered using `cleanCounts()` (minimum library size: 100)
- Feature selection via `restrictCorpus()` - genes appearing in fewer than 5% or more than 100% of spots excluded. the top 1,000 overdispersed genes retained per condition

### 2. STdeconvolve — Cell Type Deconvolution
- LDA (Latent Dirichlet Allocation) models fit across K = 2-9 topics using `fitLDA()` to determine best fit
- Optimal K=7 selected for all three conditions based on perplexity and proportion of rare cell types (though I admit that K=8 is better for 12h)
- Cell-type proportions ($\theta$) and transcriptional profiles ($\beta$) extracted via `getBetaTheta()`
- Topics annotated using known kidney cell-type marker genes:
  - Pod (Podocytes), PTs12 / PTs12\_1 / PTs12\_2 (Straight Proximal Tubule), PTs3 / PTs3\_TAL (Convoluted Proximal Tubule), TAL / TAL\_1 (Thick Ascending Limb), DCT (Distal Convoluted Tubule), Immune
  - Note: we did not identify every part of the kidney. Some of the injured version might dominate and erase the signal of cell types entirely, making it look like that cell type disappeared. 
- Spatial maps of cell-type proportions plotted using `scatterbar`
- Average marker expression heatmaps generated per condition to confirm topic annotations

### 3. De Novo Transcriptional Induction
- Genes with zero or near-zero expression at baseline (Sham) identified
- Module scores for de novo activated genes computed per spot across conditions
- Spatial maps and density plots show the global shift from transcriptional silence (Sham) to peak activation (AKI 12h)

### 4. Differential Expression & Volcano Plot
- Pseudobulk differential expression performed comparing AKI conditions to Sham
- Significance threshold: adjusted p-value < 0.05 and |log2FC| > 1
- Top upregulated markers of acute cortical stress labeled (e.g., *Havcr1*, *Krt20*, *Lcn2*, *Serpine1*, *Cdkn1a*)

### 5. Integrated Damage Score
- Aggregated expression of the top 10 DE markers into a per-spot SCT module score
- Spatial maps show injury epicenter localization; violin plots with pairwise t-tests confirm statistically significant escalation of damage over time (all comparisons p < 0.0001)

### 6. Macrophage Analysis
- Macrophage presence assessed via *Cd68* expression across conditions
- Macrophage marker score computed per spot using canonical markers: *Lyz2*, *Ctss*, *Cd52*, *Tyrobp*, *C1qc*, *C1qa*, *C1qb*, *Apoe*, *Cfh*, *Cd74*, *Csf1r*, *Adgre1*, *Cx3cr1*, *Spp1*, *Cd68*
- Corticomedullary junction (CMJ) estimated from cell-type proportions. The macrophage expression plotted as a function of distance from CMJ
- Spearman correlation computed between macrophage score and each deconvolved cell-type proportion per condition
- Pairwise Wilcoxon tests (Bonferroni corrected) confirm significant macrophage accumulation escalation across timepoints

### 7. Spatial Statistics
- **Bivariate LISA (Local Indicators of Spatial Association):** identifies spots where tissue damage and immune signals co-localize
- **Moran's I:** tracks spatial autocorrelation of *Spp1*, *Sox9*, *Serpine1*, and *Tgfb1* across timepoints, which confirms emergence of organized injury and repair niches

### 8. GO Enrichment Analysis
- Top 100 marker genes per STdeconvolve topic extracted
- Gene symbols converted to Entrez IDs using `bitr()` with the `org.Mm.eg.db` mouse annotation database
- GO Biological Process enrichment run via `enrichGO()` (BH correction, p < 0.05)
- KEGG pathway enrichment run via `enrichKEGG()` (organism: `mmu`)
- Enrichment performed across all cell types for all three conditions

### 9. CellChat (Cell-Cell Communication)
- Dominant cell-type label per Visium spot assigned from STdeconvolve θ matrix (argmax)
- `CellChat` used to infer ligand-receptor interactions
- **SPP1 signaling network** (immune recruitment) and **TGF- $\beta$ signaling network** (repair/fibrosis) examined across Sham, 4h, and 12h
- Dot plots of recruitment signals targeting immune cells shown per condition
- Pathway information flow (total communication probability) compared across all timepoints

### 10. Pseudotime Analysis
- Injury timeline score (0-100) derived from de novo activation gene module scores
- Density distributions of timeline scores per condition confirm sequential biological progression
- Trajectory of *Havcr1* (acute injury), *Sox9* (cell fate/regeneration), and *Serpine1* / *Tgfb1* (fibrotic signaling) plotted along the pseudotime axis
- Spatial association between damage (*Spp1*) and repair (*Sox9*) signals examined in neighboring spots via quartile-stratified boxplots
- **6-week validation:** Havcr1 resolution, Sox9 failure, and permanent cortical fibrosis confirmed, which was consistent with AKI-to-CKD transition predicted by the 12h model

## Key Findings

1. **De novo transcriptional induction** is near-absent at baseline and erupts globally by 12h, with the injury epicenter localizing to the outer cortex and the corticomedullary junction (CMJ).
2. **Top injury markers** *Havcr1*, *Krt20*, *Lcn2*, *Serpine1*, *Cdkn1a* are strongly upregulated by 4h and peak at 12h.
3. **Injured Cell types** Straight Proximal Tubule cells start to get injured at 4h and are the most injured by 12h. The Thick acscending limb joins them at the 12h mark, making the injury most server around the CMJ.
4.  **Metabolic Pathway** Enrichment analysis showed that Proximal tubule cells started going through glycolysis at 4h to surive the ischemia (lack of oxygen for electron transport chain). The injured TAL cells start coegulation regulation and are showing response to damage. 
5. **Macrophages** (Spp1+/CD68+/Ccr2+) accumulate significantly by 12h and preferentially co-localize with the Injured\_TAL cell population at the CMJ.
6. **SPP1 signaling** from injured tubules and TAL cells drives macrophage recruitment in a spatially organized manner. This is interesting as the structural damage is primarily in the straight proximal tubules, but immune orchestration is delegated to the TAL. 
7. **TGF-$\beta$ / fibrotic signaling** rises in parallel with injury, competing with the regenerative Sox9 program with the fibrotic trajectory dominates by 12h and is confirmed established at 6 weeks.

---

## Limitations

- **Visium resolution (55 $\mu$ m spots):** Each spot captures a mixture of multiple cell types, creating confounded signals between macrophages and injured epithelial cells.
- **No pure macrophage LDA topic emerged** because macrophages are spatially intermingled with injured tubules rather than forming discrete clusters.
- **Dominant-label assignment for CellChat** (argmax of $\theta$) discards the continuous mixture information from LDA, potentially missing intra-spot co-localized signaling or misattributing signals to the dominant cell type.
